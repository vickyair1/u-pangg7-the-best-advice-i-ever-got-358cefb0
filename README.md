# The Best Advice I Ever Got

An entry for [make.fun's "The Best Advice I Ever Got" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pangg7-the-best-advice-i-ever-got-358cefb0): one sentence, up to 200 characters, about the best advice ever received, judged in pairwise blind rounds until the line that carries the most lived truth wins.

## The line

> My dad told me: never trust a man who won't admit he's wrong — twenty years on, I still check for that first.

Dad's test for trustworthiness — a man who can't admit he's wrong — and the turn is that twenty years later the writer still applies it first, before anything else. Specific, earned, a real voice recalling a real moment; no slogan, no fortune cookie.

## How it works

`main.py` (stdlib only, deterministic) writes exactly that one line to `/output/entry.txt` — no trailing newline. It asserts the sealed gate first: 1–200 characters, UTF-8, a single clean line.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
